# docs/research — purpose and rules

This folder holds **point-in-time research surveys** that inform requirements, architecture, and design. Surveys are dated snapshots: they are never edited after publication — a newer survey supersedes by reference, not by rewrite.

## Conventions

- Filename: `RS-<kebab-topic>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`.
- Each survey states its research date, what was checked, and the fit assessment for our setting at that time.
- `RS-choices-<date>.md` records the options on the table and what was selected, deferred, or ruled out — with reasons — so later readers can see why, not just what.
- No customer identifiers, no operator paths, no `tmp/` references.
