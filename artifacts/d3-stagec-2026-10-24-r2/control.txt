#### 6.2g Shadow-off control arm (F-PROBE-07)

Because unit-level non-interference (F01 differentials) is
necessary but not sufficient live evidence, the probe
includes a small control arm: the same fixtures run with
`IAP_SHADOW_TYPED_DEFINITIONS` unset (default off).
Control-arm sizing (decided): exactly **one control run per
fixture exercised in the shadow arm**, and never more
control runs than shadow attempts on the same fixture —
the control cohort mirrors the shadow cohort 1:1 at minimum
and cannot exceed it. Control runs compare native outcomes,
error behavior, and the *absence* of shadow artifacts (no
additive keys, no shadow telemetry). Control runs are
ordinary flag-off runs: they change nothing native and
require no authority. They are excluded from the
eligible-turn denominator (they carry no typed observations
by construction) and reported alongside, as the bounded
live complement to the unit differentials. If a control run
and its shadow counterpart diverge natively, the probe
stops: that is a non-interference failure, not a parity
signal.
