## (v1.9 §6.2b cohort and run-level eligibility)

#### 6.2b Cohort and run-level eligibility (F-PROBE-02)

- Eligible fixtures: the code-bearing L-series battery cases
  present in the repo at freeze time, enumerated by id in the
  frozen manifest (no silent substitution; a fixture added
  later starts a new run set).
- A run is *eligible* iff it completes without platform error
  AND evaluates at least one definition-claim candidate at the
  verify gate under `verifier_version == v2` AND records a
  native outcome for that candidate. Runs that complete with
  zero definition-claim candidates are *non-eligible runs*:
  counted separately, reported, and excluded from the
  denominator — never retroactively reclassified.
- Objective exclusion rules (applied identically to every
  run, pre-registered, never decided post hoc): turn-contained
  platform errors and timeouts whose error outcome was
  recorded through the normal channel (excluded attempts,
  budget consumed; see error-class partition above);
  non-v2 evaluations; runs whose telemetry or conclude
  result is missing for reasons other than a counted
  capture event (treated as ambiguous joins — they void
  the run set per §6.1); runs executed outside the frozen
  environment. Platform or infrastructure errors that are
  *not* contained and recorded as above — worker crash or
  restart, store unreachable, telemetry pipeline failure,
  ambiguous ordering or identity — are not exclusions;
  they void the run set.

## (A1-1 timeout disposition (OD-01))

## A1-1. v1.9 §6.5 item 7 amended — timeout disposition (OD-01)

"An UNRECORDED timeout — or any timeout that prevents the outcome from being
recorded, corrupts ordering/identity/persistence/the frozen environment, or
whose containment cannot be proven from the record — voids the affected runs
(retain artifacts, mark INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, never trim/combine/rerun under
that identity; replacement only by new freeze, new run-set ID, newly
authorized budget). A RECORDED turn-contained timeout whose error outcome
arrived through the normal channel with `producing_path` and a joinable
envelope is an excluded attempt per §6.1 (budget consumed, collection
continues)."

## (A1-2 superseded-attempt disposition (OD-02))

## A1-2. v1.9 §6.5 item 4 amended — superseded-attempt disposition (OD-02)

"A superseded-attempt observation that is merely present is EXCLUDED from the
denominator and counted separately per §1.4, never joined, and does not void
by presence. An attempted cross-execution join of a superseded observation to
another execution's native result, or inability to prove accepted-attempt
identity, is an ambiguous join and voids the run set."
