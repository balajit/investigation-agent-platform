## (v1.9 §6.2e probe budget and go/no-go)

#### 6.2e Probe budget and go/no-go (F-PROBE-05)

- Pilot budget (decided): at most **12 *attempted* runs per
  decision-7 cell** (owner-adjustable only by re-freeze, never
  mid-collection). "Attempted" is deliberate: eligibility is
  applied *after* the run, so excluded runs (non-eligible,
  errored, voided) still consume budget. Repeated exclusions
  cannot turn a nominally bounded pilot into unbounded
  collection — when a cell's 12 attempts are spent, the cell
  is closed as populated or not.
  Collection stops early if all four cells reach populatable
  status sooner; unfilled budget is not spent to "improve"
  numbers.
- Go/no-go (no discretion): the probe *passes* (formal
  collection may be proposed) iff all four decision-7 cells
  are demonstrated populatable within budget AND the
  subcategory presence rule (§6.1) is either satisfied or
  explicitly routed to restricted conclusions AND zero
  capture errors occurred in the counted runs. Otherwise
  the outcome is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE by construction —
  the protocol never lowers the floor to fit the data, and
  the floor is never met by re-running until cells fill.
- Budget accounting for voids, retries, and replacements
  (D3-V18-03 — no implicit refills, no salvage by trimming
  or combining):
  - A retry IS an attempt and consumes the same frozen
    budget as a first try; there is no separate retry
    allowance, and a retry never resets or extends the cap.
  - A voided run set is terminal for that collection: it
    cannot be salvaged by trimming failed records, dropping
    inconvenient turns, or combining surviving turns with a
    replacement set. Trimmed or combined run sets are
    INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE by construction, not a pass.
  - A clean replacement after a void requires a NEW
    collection under a NEW freeze (new snapshot identity,
    new run-set id): previously consumed attempts do not
    transfer, and the new collection receives its own
    explicitly authorized budget — never an automatic
    refill of the old one.
  - Within-budget reruns after *excluded* (non-void)
    attempts — e.g. a run with zero definition-claim
    candidates, or a platform error that voids only its own
    run — do NOT require a re-freeze, provided nothing
    else changed (same code, flags, fixtures, prompts,
    tenant, harness). Any change beyond the mere rerun
    (new code, new flags, new fixtures, new prompts, new
    tenant, new harness version) is a material change and
    triggers a new freeze.

## (A1-3 reservation buckets (BUD-01))

## A1-3. v1.9 §6.2e cap semantics clarified — reservation buckets (BUD-01)

The "12 attempted runs per decision cell" cap applies to pre-run
reservations against owner-frozen expected-cell buckets, with a hard
run-set-total limit of 48 reservations. Each attempt is charged exactly once
to its frozen expected-cell bucket at reservation time. An attempt whose
observed outcome maps to a different cell is recorded as EXCLUDED against the
reserved bucket (reason `BUDGET_RESERVED_CELL_MISMATCH`) and is never
re-attributed, refunded, or transferred. Observed-cell counts are reported
separately as diagnostic data and are not subject to the 12-cap; no parity,
coverage, or pass claim may be derived from exceeding observed counts.
Post-hoc reassignment, `UNKNOWN_CELL` pools, target-cell guessing, and
refunds are forbidden. This clarification changes cap semantics versus a
strict observed-cell reading and is adopted here by explicit owner decision.
